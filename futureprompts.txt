-instead of drawing each spiral from one end one at a time, have each spiral generate in parallel, starting from both ends at once. If there are 12 panels, this should be a 24x speedup because each spiral is being drawn at once from both ends. This also means that the shape of the spiral must be predefined for each run before drawing starts to make sure that both sides align correctly.
-when on the outward pass, instead of filling the gap created by a child in a single 180 deg arc, fill it across the full 360 degs before the child. (so the current arc being used and the arc drawn just before it) This should not create any intersections.
-decrease the stroke width of 2nd degree recursions to 0.75 and 3rd degree to 0.5, and decrease stroke width proportionally for the thin screen version.
-create a new color option that instead of having the color interpolate over the x-coordinate of the stroke, have it interpolate over the distance of the line. Since the line shape is now deterministic, you know the full length of the line ahead of time and can determine how quickly to change the color. Have this option enabled by default.
